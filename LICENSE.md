# License (TBD)

JodKit is intended to be open source. **No license has been selected yet.**

Candidates under discussion include MIT and AGPL. Criteria include community adoption, commercial ecosystem, SaaS usage, plugin marketplace, hosted offerings, and protection against closed forks.

See [docs/project-foundation-v0.1.md section 44](docs/project-foundation-v0.1.md#44-open-source) and [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md#decisions-not-yet-final).

Until a license is chosen, do not assume redistribution terms beyond what your jurisdiction allows for unpublished design documents.
